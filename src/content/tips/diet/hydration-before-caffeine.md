---
title: "Drink Water Before Your First Coffee"
pillar: "diet"
summary: "A tiny reordering of your morning that makes a real difference in energy and focus."
date: 2026-01-02
tags: ["hydration", "morning"]
---

You wake up dehydrated after hours without water — and reaching straight for caffeine on top of that can leave you jittery instead of genuinely alert. Try a full glass of water first thing, before the coffee.

It's a small swap, but it resets the order of your morning around your body's actual needs instead of just habit. Keep a glass by your bed if that makes it easier to remember.
