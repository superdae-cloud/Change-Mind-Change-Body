Before you decide what else is on your plate, decide on your protein: eggs, chicken, fish, beans, tofu, Greek yogurt — whatever fits your life and your budget. Protein keeps you full longer, supports muscle repair, and steadies your energy through the day.

You don't need to count grams to benefit from this. Just make sure every meal has a protein source you can name, and let the rest of the plate fill in around it.
