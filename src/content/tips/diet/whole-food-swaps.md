Change isn't always about subtraction — sometimes it's just substitution. Try these three: swap sugary cereal for oats with fruit, swap soda for sparkling water, swap white bread for a whole-grain option you actually enjoy.

None of these require willpower marathons. Pick one, make it automatic, then move to the next. That's how sustainable change actually works.
