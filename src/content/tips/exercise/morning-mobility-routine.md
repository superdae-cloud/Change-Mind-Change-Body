Before your feet hit the floor, give your body five minutes of attention. Ankle circles, hip openers, a few slow spinal twists, shoulder rolls. Nothing intense — just enough to tell your joints "we're moving today."

Do this consistently and you'll notice fewer aches, better posture, and a body that's ready to work instead of one you have to fight into gear. It costs nothing but five minutes, and it's the easiest habit on this list to keep.
