title: "What 'Progressive Overload' Actually Means for You"
pillar: "exercise"
summary: "The one training principle behind almost every strength gain you'll ever make."
date: 2025-12-20
tags: ["strength", "training-basics"]
---

Progressive overload just means this: your body only changes when you ask more of it than it's used to. That could be one more rep, a little more weight, a little more time, or a little better form.

You don't need to chase this every single session — small, steady increases over weeks and months are what build real strength. Track what you did last time, and try to beat it, even by a little. That's the whole system.
