You don't need a workout plan to move more — you need a reason to stand up after you eat. A short walk after a meal helps your body manage blood sugar more efficiently and takes the edge off that post-meal sluggishness.

Start with one meal a day. Doesn't matter how far or how fast — just get moving. Once it's automatic, add it to a second meal. This is the definition of a habit that compounds.
