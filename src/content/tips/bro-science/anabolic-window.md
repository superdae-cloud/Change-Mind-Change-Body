The gym-floor rule is that you have a tiny window right after your workout to get protein in, or your gains are wasted. Research doesn't back the "30-minute sprint to the shaker bottle." What matters far more is your total daily protein intake and consistency over weeks and months.

If you've eaten a normal meal within a couple hours before training, your body still has plenty of amino acids on board. Have your post-workout meal when it's convenient — just don't skip protein for the day entirely.
