A large portion of your immune tissue is located in your digestive tract, which means what you feed your gut has a direct line to how well your immune system functions. Fiber-rich foods, fermented foods like yogurt or sauerkraut, and variety in your plant intake all support a healthier gut microbiome.

You don't need an elaborate protocol — just more plants, more variety, and a little fermented food when you can work it in.
