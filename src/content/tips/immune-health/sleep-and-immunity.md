During deep sleep, your body produces and releases proteins that help fight infection and inflammation. Consistently cutting sleep short doesn't just make you tired — it measurably weakens your immune response.

Aim for a consistent sleep and wake time, even on weekends. Consistency matters as much as total hours. This is the most underrated habit on this entire site.
