Ending your shower with 30 seconds of cold water is one of the simplest ways to build resilience and has been linked to improved circulation and immune markers over time. Start small — you're not training for a polar plunge, you're training your nervous system to handle discomfort without panic.

Build up the duration as it becomes easier. The physical benefit is real, but the mental one — proving to yourself you can handle discomfort on purpose — might matter even more.
