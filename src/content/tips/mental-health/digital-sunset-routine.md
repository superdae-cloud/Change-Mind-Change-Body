---
title: "Build a 'Digital Sunset' Into Your Evening"
pillar: "mental-health"
summary: "Set a nightly cutoff for screens and give your mind room to actually wind down."
date: 2025-12-22
tags: ["sleep", "screens", "evening-routine"]
---

Pick a time, 30-60 minutes before bed, when screens go away — phone, TV, laptop, all of it. Use that window for something low-stimulation: reading, stretching, journaling, or just talking with someone in the room.

Your mind needs a runway to land before sleep, not a cliff edge. This one habit alone tends to improve both sleep quality and next-day mood more than people expect.
