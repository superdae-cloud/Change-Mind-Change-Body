Inhale for four counts, hold for four, exhale for four, hold for four. Repeat for a minute or two. That's the entire technique — and it works because it directly calms your nervous system, not just your thoughts.

Use it before a hard conversation, in the middle of a stressful moment, or anytime your mind is racing faster than you'd like. No equipment, no app required, works anywhere.
