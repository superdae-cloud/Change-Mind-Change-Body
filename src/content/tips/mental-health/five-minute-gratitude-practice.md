Before you check your phone, write down three things you're grateful for. They don't need to be profound — "good coffee," "a warm bed," and "a friend who texted back" all count.

This isn't about ignoring what's hard. It's about training your attention to notice what's already working, so you walk into the day from a place of steadiness instead of scarcity. Five minutes, a notebook, done.
