Omega-3 fatty acids support heart, brain, and joint health, but most diets lean heavily toward omega-6s and light on omega-3s. Fatty fish like salmon and sardines are the best food source — if that's not a regular part of your meals, a fish oil or algae-based supplement is a reasonable way to fill the gap.

As always: this supports a good diet, it doesn't replace one.
