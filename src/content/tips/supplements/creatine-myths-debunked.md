Creatine doesn't damage your kidneys in healthy individuals, it isn't just for bodybuilders, and it doesn't require a "loading phase" to work. A consistent daily dose of about 3-5 grams supports strength, muscle performance, and even some cognitive benefits over time.

It's cheap, well-studied, and one of the few supplements with genuinely strong evidence behind it. Consistency matters more than timing — take it daily, and give it a few weeks.
